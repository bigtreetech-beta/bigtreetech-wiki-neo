## Product Overview

The BMCU is a multi-material control system based on open-source designs, engineered to replicate AMS functionality. It is compatible with Bambu Lab A-series, X-series, and P-series printers (Note: The P2S is not currently supported). Designed as a 4-channel system, the BMCU utilizes a CH32 microcontroller. Please note that RFID material identification is not currently supported.

As a third-party DIY accessory, the BMCU's stability may differ from the official AMS Lite, and future official printer firmware updates may impact compatibility. The BMCU is under active development; subsequent releases of firmware or structural parts may require manual user upgrades.

The BMCU is intended for DIY enthusiasts and educational purposes. Special thanks to the original creator, 4061N. BIQU is the exclusive authorized commercial manufacturer for the BMCU project.

## Product Features

- Faster Filament Switching: Unlike the AMCU, filament unloading does not require full retraction back to the runout sensor. It only needs to retract to the 5-way connector, significantly speeding up the switching process.
- Direct Dry Box Feeding: The parallel design operates without a built-in spool holder, allowing you to feed filament directly from a sealed dry box to the printer.
- Active Filament Buffering: An integrated buffer mechanism actively feeds filament before the printer detects a tangle, preventing extrusion issues caused by excessive pulling resistance.
- Standardized Hardware: The design utilizes standardized components with unified specifications for many mechanical parts. This reduces additional costs and ensures a high success rate for one-time DIY assembly.
- Advanced Photoelectric Detection: The BMCU features a photoelectric detection system capable of sensing transparent filaments. This eliminates the drag and instability commonly associated with microswitch sensors. This photoelectric module will also be released separately under an open-source license.

## About this guide

This guide provides step-by-step instructions for assembling the Panda BMCU-370 DIY kit. Before you begin, ensure you have 3D-printed all the necessary parts as outlined in the product documentation.This guide explains how to assemble the BMCU DIY kit. Before you begin, 3D-print the required parts. You will assemble them with the components included in the kit.

## Assembly requirements

The parts are small. Work on a large, empty table to avoid losing them. Some soldering is required, so basic soldering experience is necessary.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-3.webp').default} width="50%"/>

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-4.webp').default} width="50%"/>

5. Prepare two 182A gears and two 2*20mm shafts. Install each gear onto a shaft.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-5.webp').default} width="50%"/>

6. Prepare the BMG gear with a set screw and the 5mm shaft. Slide the BMG gear onto the shaft.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-6.webp').default} width="50%"/>

7. Prepare two MR85 bearings.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-7.webp').default} width="50%"/>

8. Fit the bearings onto the 5x22mm shaft. Make sure the bearing on the BMG gear side is flush with the end of the shaft.9.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-8.webp').default} width="50%"/>

9. Once the end is flush, tighten the BMG set screw.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-9.webp').default} width="50%"/>

10. Attach the radial magnet to the top of the gear, keeping it roughly centered.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-10.webp').default} width="50%"/>

11. Prepare the buffer slider and one 3*20mm magnet. Alternatively, stack two 3*10mm magnets together.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-11.webp').default} width="50%"/>

12. Press the magnet into the printed buffer slider with the S pole facing upward. To identify the S pole, float the magnet on water and allow it to rotate freely; the end that turns to point toward geographic south is the S pole. Alternatively, the S pole is the one that attracts the north‑pointing end of a compass needle (the red or marked end).

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-12.webp').default} width="50%"/>

13. After pressing the magnet in, tighten the M2x8 screw.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-13.webp').default} width="50%"/>

14. Prepare the printed lever, the BMG gear without a set screw, two needle bearings, and the 3mm shaft from the BMG kit.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-14.webp').default} width="50%"/>

15. Insert the two needle bearings into the BMG gear.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-15.webp').default} width="50%"/>

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-16.webp').default} width="50%"/>

17. Press the 3mm shaft into the lever from one side. Make sure the holes in the lever and BMG gear remain aligned.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-17.webp').default} width="50%"/>

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-18.webp').default} width="50%"/>

18. Prepare the worm gear and the 370 motor. Press the worm gear onto the motor shaft, taking care not to damage it. After installation, apply grease to the worm gear to reduce wear during operation.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-19.webp').default} width="50%"/>

19. Tin the ends of two wires, then solder them to the terminals at the rear of the 370 motor.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-21.webp').default} width="50%"/>

Make sure the holes are aligned.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-22.webp').default} width="50%"/>

After installation, the assembly should look as shown.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-23.webp').default} width="50%"/>

21. Install the two previously assembled 182A gears and the BMG gear with the set screw. Make sure the magnet on the BMG gear faces upward. If the 182A gears are not fully seated against the stops in the rear cover, press them down until fully seated.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-24.webp').default} width="50%"/>

22. Prepare the 5mm steel ball and the printed filament runout optical flag, then install them into the rear cover in sequence.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-25.webp').default} width="50%"/>

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-kit-28.webp').default} width="50%"/>
