## Product Overview

The BMCU is a multi-material control system based on open-source designs, engineered to replicate AMS functionality. It is compatible with Bambu Lab A-series, X-series, and P-series printers (Note: The P2S is not currently supported). Designed as a 4-channel system, the BMCU utilizes a CH32 microcontroller. Please note that RFID material identification is not currently supported.

As a third-party DIY accessory, the BMCU's stability may differ from the official AMS Lite, and future official printer firmware updates may impact compatibility. The BMCU is under active development; subsequent releases of firmware or structural parts may require manual user upgrades.

The BMCU is intended for DIY enthusiasts and educational purposes. Special thanks to the original creator, 4061N. BIQU is the exclusive authorized commercial manufacturer for the BMCU project.

## Product Features

- Faster Filament Switching: Unlike the AMCU, filament unloading does not require full retraction back to the runout sensor. It only needs to retract to the 5-way connector, significantly speeding up the switching process.
- Direct Dry Box Feeding: The parallel design operates without a built-in spool holder, allowing you to feed filament directly from a sealed dry box to the printer.
- Active Filament Buffering: An integrated buffer mechanism actively feeds filament before the printer detects a tangle, preventing extrusion issues caused by excessive pulling resistance.
- Standardized Hardware: The design utilizes standardized components with unified specifications for many mechanical parts. This reduces additional costs and ensures a high success rate for one-time DIY assembly.
- Advanced Photoelectric Detection: The BMCU features a photoelectric detection system capable of sensing transparent filaments. This eliminates the drag and instability commonly associated with microswitch sensors. This photoelectric module will also be released separately under an open-source license.

## About this guide

This guide explains how to assemble the auto-rewind spool holder for the BMCU DIY kit. Print the required models first, then assemble them with the parts included in the DIY kit.

## Before Assembly

The parts are small and easy to misplace. Assemble them on a clear, spacious work surface. Wear gloves when bending or cutting the wire to help prevent injury.

## Auto-Rewind Spool Holder Assembly

The image below shows the printed parts required for one auto-rewind spool holder. The rotary core marked `1` comes in standard and mirrored versions. Make sure you use the correct version during assembly.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-1.webp').default} width="40%"/>

1. Identify the spool retaining arm and its base as shown below.

:::info[Note]

NOTE: The retaining arm is available in two sizes. Use the small arm for standard filament spools. Use the large arm if the spool hole is larger.

:::

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-2.webp').default} width="40%"/>

2. Apply glue to the base.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-3.webp').default} width="40%"/>

3. Fit the retaining arm onto the base and allow the glue time to completely dry.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-4.webp').default} width="40%"/>

4. Assemble three retaining arm assemblies as shown.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-5.webp').default} width="40%"/>

5. Prepare the bending jig, torsion spring, and pliers.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-6.webp').default} width="40%"/>

6. Fit the bending jig over the torsion spring. Mark the spring wire at the point where it exits the jig.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-7.webp').default} width="40%"/>

7. Cut the spring wire at the mark.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-8.webp').default} width="40%"/>

8. Trim the wire on the other side in the same way.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-9.webp').default} width="40%"/>

9. After trimming, make sure the two spring legs are the same length, as shown.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-10.webp').default} width="40%"/>

10. Place the trimmed torsion spring into the bending jig in the position shown.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-11.webp').default} width="40%"/>

11. Bend the spring leg over the edge as shown.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-12.webp').default} width="40%"/>

12. Continue bending the leg around the jig as far as it will go.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-13.webp').default} width="40%"/>

13. Repeat the same bending process on the other spring leg.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-14.webp').default} width="40%"/>

14.Remove the torsion spring. Use the pliers to shape the bent leg so it retains a U shape after the pliers are released.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-15.webp').default} width="40%"/>

15. Shape the other spring leg in the same way. The finished spring should look as shown.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-16.webp').default} width="40%"/>

16. Prepare the rotary core and clamping block.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-17.webp').default} width="40%"/>

17. Insert the clamping block into the rotary core from one end.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-18.webp').default} width="40%"/>

18. After insertion, make sure the resulting hexagonal profile at the bottom is symmetrical, as shown.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-19.webp').default} width="40%"/>

19. Prepare the rotary shaft, torsion spring, and rotary core.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-20.webp').default} width="40%"/>

20. Insert the rotary core into the rotary shaft from the end with the locating post, as shown.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-21.webp').default} width="40%"/>

21. Install the torsion spring in the position shown.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-22.webp').default} width="40%"/>

22. The assembled standard and mirrored versions should look as shown.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-23.webp').default} width="40%"/>
<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-24.webp').default} width="40%"/>

23. Apply grease to the locations shown on the rotary shaft. You may also apply it after installing the cap in the next step.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-25.webp').default} width="40%"/>

24. Prepare the rotary shaft cap.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-26.webp').default} width="40%"/>

25. Install the cap on the end with the rotary core, then turn it counterclockwise to tighten.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-27.webp').default} width="40%"/>
<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-28.webp').default} width="40%"/>

26. Prepare the rotary shaft housing.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-29.webp').default} width="40%"/>

27. Insert the rotary shaft into the housing.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-30.webp').default} width="40%"/>

28. After installation, make sure the rotary shaft sits flush with the housing, as shown.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-31.webp').default} width="40%"/>

29. Prepare the washer retaining ring and wave washer. Fit the wave washer into the retaining ring.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-32.webp').default} width="40%"/>

30. The installed wave washer should look as shown.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-33.webp').default} width="40%"/>

31. With the housing installed on the rotary shaft, fit the retaining ring and wave washer onto the shaft. Keep the ring pressed down during installation.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-34.webp').default} width="40%"/>

32. While keeping the retaining ring pressed down, turn it counterclockwise until it locks in place.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-35.webp').default} width="40%"/>

33. Insert the three retaining arms into the three slots in the rotary shaft housing, oriented as shown.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-36.webp').default} width="40%"/>

34. Once installed, the three clamping arms should look as shown.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-37.webp').default} width="40%"/>

35. Prepare the fixed shaft base.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-38.webp').default} width="40%"/>

36. Insert the fixed shaft base into the bottom of the rotary shaft. Align the internal hexagonal shape so the base can be fully seated.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-39.webp').default} width="40%"/>

## Final Assembly

1. Assemble the two auto-rewind spool holders, using the standard rotary core for one and the mirrored rotary core for the other.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-40.webp').default} width="40%"/>

2. Prepare the spool holder base.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-41.webp').default} width="40%"/>

3. Install the two auto-rewind spool holder on the base as shown. If the rewind direction is incorrect, swap the two spool holders.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-bmcu370/img/panda-bmcu370-auto-42.webp').default} width="40%"/>
