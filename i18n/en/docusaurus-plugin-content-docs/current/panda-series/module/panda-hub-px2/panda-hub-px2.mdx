# Panda Hub PX2

## Product Overview

The BIGTREETECH Panda Hub PX2 is a compact power expansion module that provides additional power connections for accessories inside the printer chamber. Designed for Bambu Lab P2S and X2D printers, it can be installed with a printed mount and the supplied fasteners to secure and protect the printer's original USB connection from vibration and wear.

## Features

- Compact design for easy integration inside the printer chamber.
- Provides two USB Type-A ports and two 24 V XH2.54 ports.
- The printed mount for the P2S and X2D secures the Panda Hub PX2 with the supplied fasteners and helps protect the USB connection from vibration and wear.

## Important Notes

:::warning[Output Limits]

- The total 5 V output current must not exceed 1.4 A.
- The total 24 V output current must not exceed 1 A.
- The Panda Hub PX2 is designed for power delivery only and does not support standard USB data communication.

:::

## Installation Guide

:::danger[Safety]

Power off the printer and disconnect it from mains power before removing covers or disconnecting internal cables. Do not pull hard when handling the factory wiring and connectors.

:::

1. **Install the Panda Hub PX2 power module.** Secure the module to the printed mount with the supplied M3 self-tapping screws.

    <ImageView

        alt="Panda Hub PX2 power module secured to the printed mount"
        width="50%"
    />

2. **Install the Panda Hub PX2.** Clip the Panda Hub PX2 into the mounting slot above the printer's front frame.

    <ImageView
        src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-hub-px2/img/hub-installed.webp').default}
        alt="Panda Hub PX2 installed above the printer's front frame"
        width="50%"
    />

3. **Open the printer's left-side cover.** Carefully open the cover and pull out the connector shown. Do not pull hard, as excessive force may damage the factory wiring.

    <ImageView
        src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-hub-px2/img/side-cover-connector.webp').default}
        alt="Connector pulled out from behind the printer's left-side cover"
        width="35%"
    />

4. **Disconnect the innermost connector.** Disconnect the connector shown in the image.

    <ImageView
        src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-hub-px2/img/inner-connector-location.webp').default}
        alt="Location of the innermost connector to disconnect"
        width="50%"
    />

5. **Connect the factory plug to the Y-splitter.** Connect the plug removed from the printer to the white receptacle on the supplied Y-splitter cable.

    <ImageView
        src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-hub-px2/img/y-splitter-factory-plug.webp').default}
        alt="Factory plug connected to the white Y-splitter receptacle"
        width="50%"
    />

6. **Connect the Y-splitter to the printer mainboard.** Insert the black plug into the printer mainboard, reconnect the factory cable as shown, and close the side cover.

    <ImageView
        src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-hub-px2/img/y-splitter-mainboard.webp').default}
        alt="Y-splitter connected to the printer mainboard"
        width="50%"
    />

7. **Route and secure the cables.** Arrange and secure the cables as shown, making sure they cannot interfere with any moving parts.

    <ImageView
        src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-hub-px2/img/installation-complete.webp').default}
        alt="Completed Panda Hub PX2 installation and cable routing"
        width="50%"
    />

:::info[Installation Check]

Before restoring power, confirm that all connectors are fully seated and that the cable harness cannot enter the print-head travel area.

:::
