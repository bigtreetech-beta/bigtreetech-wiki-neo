## Product Overview

The BIGTREETECH Panda Hub PX2 is a compact power expansion module that provides additional power connections for accessories inside the printer chamber. Designed for Bambu Lab P2S and X2D printers, it can be installed with a printed mount and the supplied fasteners to secure and protect the printer's original USB connection from vibration and wear.

## Technical Specifications

| Specification  | Panda Hub PX2                         |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Power output interface   | 2 x USB Type-A; 2 x 24V XH2.54      |
| 5V total output current | Maximum 1.4A                        |
| 24V total output current | Maximum 1A                          |
| USB data communication   | Not supported, only for power supply |

</div>

## Features

- Compact design for easy integration inside the printer chamber.
- Provides two USB Type-A ports and two 24 V XH2.54 ports.
- The printed mount for the P2S and X2D secures the Panda Hub PX2 with the supplied fasteners and helps protect the USB connection from vibration and wear.

## Important Notes

:::warning[Output Limits]

- The total 5 V output current must not exceed 1.4 A.
- The total 24 V output current must not exceed 1 A.
- The Panda Hub PX2 is designed for power delivery only and does not support standard USB data communication.

:::

## Installation Guide

:::danger[Safety]

Power off the printer and disconnect it from mains power before removing covers or disconnecting internal cables. Do not pull hard when handling the factory wiring and connectors.

:::

### Prepare to print the mounting base

Download and print [Panda Hub PX2 mount.STL](https://github.com/bigtreetech/Panda-Hub-PX2/tree/master/3D%20model/Printed%20Parts)

We recommend using ABS or PETG for printing.

### Install Panda Hub PX2 power supply module

Secure the module to the printer mount using the included M3 self-tapping screws.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-hub-px2/img/hub-module-components.webp').default} width="45%" />

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-hub-px2/img/hub-module-mounted.webp').default} width="45%" />

### Install Panda Hub PX2

Insert the Panda Hub PX2 into the mounting slot above the front frame of the printer.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-hub-px2/img/hub-installed.webp').default} width="45%" />

### Open the left side cover of the printer

Carefully open the cover and pull out the connector shown in the diagram. Do not pull forcefully to avoid damaging the original cable.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-hub-px2/img/side-cover-connector.webp').default} width="45%" />

### Disconnect the innermost connector.

Disconnect the connector shown in the diagram.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-hub-px2/img/inner-connector-location.webp').default} width="45%" />

### Connect the original plug to the Y-type splitter.

Connect the plug unplugged from the printer to the white socket on the included Y-type splitter.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-hub-px2/img/y-splitter-factory-plug.webp').default} width="45%" />

### Connect the Y-type splitter to the printer motherboard.

Insert the black plug into the printer motherboard, reconnect the original cable as shown in the diagram, and then close the side cover.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-hub-px2/img/y-splitter-mainboard.webp').default} width="45%" />

### Organize and secure the cables

Arrange and secure the cables as shown in the diagram, ensuring that the cables do not interfere with any moving parts.

<ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-hub-px2/img/installation-complete.webp').default} width="45%" />

:::info[Installation Check]

Before restoring power, confirm that all connectors are fully seated and that the cable harness cannot enter the print-head travel area.

:::
