## Overview

**Panda Insta-Dry Spool — All-Metal Filament Spool**

    Panda Insta-Dry Spool is a detachable all-metal filament spool designed for both everyday printing and high-temperature filament drying. It supports common materials such as PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, and TPU, as well as high-performance materials such as PA, PC, and PPA that demand higher drying temperatures. Its heat-resistant construction, quick-release design, filament identification support, optional low-resistance bearing, and lightweight open-frame structure make filament changes, drying, and printing more flexible and convenient.

    - **High-Temperature Design**

        The all-metal construction tolerates higher drying temperatures, making it suitable for engineering-grade filaments that demand high-temperature drying. It also reduces the risk of softening or deformation associated with conventional plastic spools.

    - **Optional Low-Resistance Bearing**

        An optional bearing, sold separately, can be installed to reduce spool rotation resistance and provide smoother filament feeding. This is especially useful for flexible materials such as TPU and other printing scenarios that are sensitive to feed resistance.

    - **Quick-Release Design**

        The twist-lock mechanism allows the spool to be opened quickly for filament installation or replacement without complicated tools, making routine use more efficient.

    - **Broad Filament Compatibility**

        In addition to high-performance engineering filaments, the spool supports common materials such as PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, and TPU, covering a wide range of printing and drying needs.

    - **Replaceable Filament ID Chip Support**

        The spool supports RFID chip adaptation for refill filament. A Bambu Lab spool chip can be removed, installed in the matching printed part, and secured to the spool so the AMS can read and automatically match the filament information when third-party filament is used.

    - **Improved Drying Efficiency**

        The aluminum alloy provides good thermal conductivity, while the open-frame design helps transfer heat from the dryer more quickly to the spool and filament. This can help improve heating uniformity and drying efficiency. Actual results vary depending on filament type, drying equipment, and environmental conditions.

    - **Lightweight Open-Frame Design**

        The lightweight open-frame structure reduces overall weight while maintaining structural strength and gives the spool a distinctive mechanical, technical appearance.

### Filament Label/RFID Setup ([Get the Printable Part](https://makerworld.com/zh/models/3461654-panda-insta-dry-spool-rfid-print#profileId-3950083))

When you need to clearly identify the filament loaded on the spool or use a Bambu Lab RFID chip with third-party filament, you can multicolor-print this accessory and install it in the remaining-filament windows on both sides of the Panda Insta-Dry Spool. The surface text can be customized to match the filament type.

    - Customize Filament Information

        <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-insta-dry-spool/img/panda-insta-dry-spool-print.webp').default} width="80%"/>

        1. Select the printable model, then select the **text shape** from the top toolbar.

        2. When the text toolbar appears, set **Operation** to **Cut**.

        3. Enter your text in the input field, then drag it to the desired position.

        4. Select the model, then choose **Lay on Face** from the top toolbar to place the text face on the build plate.

        :::tip

        Note: If you do not need custom text, proceed directly to Step 4.

        :::

    - Install the RFID Chip

        <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-insta-dry-spool/img/panda-insta-dry-spool-print-1.webp').default} width="80%"/>

        2. Place the printed part with its back side facing up. Insert the chip into the slot as shown in the illustration. Once installed, check that the chip stays securely in place and does not fall out. For long-term use, you can secure it with tape or adhesive backing.

        3. Press the printed part until it snaps into place.

### Important Notes

    - When printing with Panda Insta-Dry Spool using an external spool holder, install the spool directly on the holder. When using the spool in the **AMS**, remove the silicone rings from the outer edges of both spool halves.

    - When drying filament with Panda Insta-Dry Spool at temperatures **above 200 $^\circ\mathrm{C}$**, remove the silicone rings from the outer edges of both spool halves. After drying, use caution when removing the spool because it may be **hot**.

    - When Panda Insta-Dry Spool is not in use, reinstall the silicone rings on the outer edges of both spool halves. This helps protect the spool edges from damage if the spool is accidentally dropped, as this could affect normal AMS operation.

    - If you are using loose filament, first place a cardboard core on the spool, then wind the filament onto it. **Note:** You can secure the filament end to the cardboard core with tape. Do not attach the filament directly to the metal inner hub, especially during high-temperature drying, as the heat may cause the filament to stick together.
