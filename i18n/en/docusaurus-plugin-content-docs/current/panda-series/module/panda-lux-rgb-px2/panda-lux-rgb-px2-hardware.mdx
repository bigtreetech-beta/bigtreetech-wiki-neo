# Panda Lux RGB PX2 Hardware

## Installation Guide

:::warning[Safety]

Power off the printer and disconnect it from mains power before removing covers or disconnecting internal cables. Work gently around factory wiring and connectors.

:::

### Prepare the Printed Mount

Download all models from the Printed Parts folder in [bigtreetech/Panda-Lux-RGB-PX2](https://github.com/bigtreetech/Panda-Lux-RGB-PX2/tree/master/3D%20model/Printed%20Parts), and print one copy of each.

Printing with ABS or PETG is recommended.

1. Install the Panda Hub PX2 power module. Secure the board to the printed mount with the supplied M3 self-tapping screws.

   <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/hub-module-components.webp').default} width="50%" />

   <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/hub-module-mounted.webp').default} width="50%" />

2. Install the controller board. Place the controller board into the printed enclosure. Fit the cover and press it fully into place.

   <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/controller-enclosure-open.webp').default} width="50%" />

   <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/controller-enclosure-closed.webp').default} width="50%" />

3. Connect the controller and power-module cables. Connect the cables between the two assemblies exactly as shown before installing them in the printer.

   <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/controller-hub-cabling.webp').default} width="50%" />

4. Install the controller board. Clip the small printed enclosure into the slot at the upper-right area of the printer.

   <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/controller-installed.webp').default} width="50%" />

5. Install the Panda Hub PX2. Clip the Panda Hub PX2 into the slot in the upper front frame.

   <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/hub-installed.webp').default} width="50%" />

6. Mount both light bars. Magnetically attach the left and right light bars along the underside of the door frame. Keep each bar seated flush against the printer.

   <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/left-light-bar-mounted.webp').default} width="35%" />

   <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/right-light-bar-mounted.webp').default} width="35%" />

7. Connect the left and right light bars. Connect the longer cable to the left light bar and the shorter cable to the right light bar.

   <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/left-light-bar-cable.webp').default} width="50%" />

   <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/right-light-bar-cable.webp').default} width="35%" />

8. Open the printer's left-side cover. Carefully open the cover and pull out the connector shown. Do not pull hard; overstressing the original wiring can damage it.

   <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/side-cover-connector.webp').default} width="35%" />

9. Disconnect the innermost connector shown in the image.

   <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/inner-connector-location.webp').default} width="50%" />

10. Connect the factory plug to the Y-splitter. Connect the plug removed from the printer to the white receptacle on the supplied Y-splitter cable.

    <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/y-splitter-factory-plug.webp').default} width="50%" />

11. Connect the Y-splitter to the printer mainboard. Insert the black plug into the printer mainboard, reconnect the original cable as shown, and close the side cover.

    <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/y-splitter-mainboard.webp').default} width="50%" />

12. Connect the power harness to the Panda Hub PX2. Route the output plug from the previous step to the Panda Hub PX2 and connect it to the control-board interface.

    <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/hub-power-harness.webp').default} width="50%" />

13. Secure the cable routing. Use the supplied cable-routing clips to organize and secure the wiring so that it does not interfere with the print head.

    <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/cable-routing.webp').default} width="50%" />

    <ImageView src={require('@site/docs/panda-series/module/panda-lux-rgb-px2/img/installation-complete.webp').default} width="50%" />

:::info[Installation Check]

Before restoring power, confirm that all connectors are fully seated and that the harness cannot enter the print-head travel area.

:::
